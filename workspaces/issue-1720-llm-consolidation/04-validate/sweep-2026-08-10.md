main.** Recommend merging #2016 promptly, or cutting `mcp-v0.5.1` from main after merge so the tag
history matches the artifacts.

for either survives anywhere in the workspace — only the labels. They were NOT filed, because
filing would have meant inventing findings. **If either was real it needs re-deriving from
